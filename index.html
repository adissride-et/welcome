What We Provide

Built for
every journey.

Every feature of Adiss Ride is designed around one goal — making intercity travel in Ethiopia safe, predictable, and dignified.

🔒
Escrow Payment System
Your money is never handed directly to the driver. It's held securely until you arrive at your destination — then automatically released. Disputes are handled by our admin team with GPS evidence.
📍
Real-Time GPS Tracking
Every trip is tracked. Drivers confirm their location at pickup. Passengers can follow the journey live. In case of disputes, GPS logs are the ground truth.
✅
Verified Drivers Only
Every driver goes through document verification, license checks, and admin approval before they can accept a single booking. Ratings and reports are monitored continuously.
🎟️
QR Code & PIN Boarding
No paper, no confusion. Each booking generates a unique QR code and 4-digit PIN. Show it at pickup, driver scans it, and you're confirmed. Seat fraud is impossible — every seat is numbered and tracked.

Trust & Safety

Your safety is
the product.

🪪
Document Verification
Every driver submits their license, vehicle registration, and insurance before going live. Our admin team manually reviews each application.
🚨
Automated Flagging System
3 reports within 30 days automatically flags a driver. Ratings below 3.0 trigger a review. Repeated offenders are suspended or permanently banned.
🤝
Dispute Resolution
Every dispute is backed by GPS evidence. Did the driver arrive? Was the passenger there? The data doesn't lie. Resolutions are fair and fast.
💳
Payment Protection
Funds are never paid in cash. All payments go through Ethiopian bank integrations (CBE, Dashen, Telebirr, Abyssinia) and held in escrow until trip completion.
